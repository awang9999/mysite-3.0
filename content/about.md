I'm a Software Engineer II on AWS HealthOmics, the AWS service for bioinformatics workflows. I work on the platform's infrastructure and tooling: new compute options, faster workflow starts, and the MCP server and VS Code extension that let bioinformaticians drive AWS from their own environments.

I graduated from Cornell in 2022 with a double major in mathematics and computer science. The math track pulled me into machine learning: I liked that the hard questions have answers you can compute. For three semesters I was a TA for Cornell's introductory ML course, CS 4780, watching the same walls show up in office hours — matrix calculus, optimization, the gap between a proof and the code. Most of this blog started as an answer to one of those questions.

I grew up in the Bay Area — Ithaca was the detour. I've been back since 2024. Away from the keyboard I cook, build Lego models, and hike or bike.
